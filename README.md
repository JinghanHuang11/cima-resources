# CIMA 学习资料仓库

本仓库包含CIMA考试各级别的学习资料。

## 📚 目录结构

### Certificate Level (证书级别)
- **BA1**: Fundamentals of Business Economics (商业经济学基础)
- **BA2**: Fundamentals of Management Accounting (管理会计基础)
- **BA3**: Fundamentals of Financial Accounting (财务会计基础)
- **BA4**: Fundamentals of Ethics, Corporate Governance and Business Law (道德、公司治理与商法基础)

### Operational Level (运营级别)
- **E1**: Organisational Management (组织管理)
- **P1**: Management Accounting (管理会计)
- **F1**: Financial Reporting and Taxation Management (财务报告与税务管理)
- **OCS**: Operational Case Study Exam (运营级别案例分析考试)

### Management Level (管理级别)
- **E2**: Project and Relationship Management (项目与关系管理)
- **P2**: Advanced Management Accounting (高级管理会计)
- **F2**: Advanced Financial Reporting (高级财务报告)
- **MCS**: Management Case Study Exam (管理级别案例分析考试)

### Strategic Level (战略级别)
- **E3**: Strategic Management (战略管理)
- **P3**: Risk Management (风险管理)
- **F3**: Financial Strategy (财务战略)
- **SCS**: Strategic Case Study Exam (战略级别案例分析考试)
